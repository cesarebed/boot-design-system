A one-page summary of every value you need to plug into Canva → Brand → Brand Kit. Click any colour swatch to copy its hex. Fonts link straight to Google Fonts for upload or selection inside Canva.

"Italy is one of the most visited countries in the world, and one of the least discovered."
Setup

How to apply this in Canva

Canva Pro is required for custom Brand Kits. You'll do this once, then every design snaps to the kit.

  1. In Canva, open Brand → Brand Kit (create one called OffTheBoot).
  2. Under Brand colours, create the four palettes below and paste each hex. Name each palette as shown.
  3. Under Brand fonts, set Young Serif for headings and Lora for body / subheadings. The wordmark uses Fraunces at weight 900 with SOFT 94.9 / WONK 1 — Canva typically doesn't expose variable font axes, so upload pre-rendered wordmark SVGs to Logos instead of trying to set Fraunces as a brand font. If Young Serif or Lora aren't in Canva's library, upload the .ttf or .otf from Google Fonts.
  4. Paste the brand voice bullets below into Brand voice so Canva's Magic Write respects the tone.
  5. Upload the OffTheBoot logo (when ready) under Logos. Use the primary on Warm White and a parchment-coloured version on Ink.

02 · Typography

Brand fonts

Three families — never introduce a fourth. Young Serif and Lora are the two you assign in Canva's Brand Kit; Fraunces is logo-only and lives in your uploaded wordmark SVGs.

03 · Voice

Brand voice

Paste these into Canva's Brand Voice field — they guide Magic Write and help anyone designing in the kit write on-brand.

We sound like this

  • Warm, direct, personal — written by a human, not a marketing team
  • "Away from the crowds"
  • "The Italy most Italians overlook"
  • "Real local knowledge"
  • Action-first CTAs: "Plan your trip", "See itineraries", "Read the story"
  • Use "slow", "local", "community", "human", "ethical"

We never sound like this

  • "Discover amazing experiences"
  • "Unforgettable adventures"
  • "Best-in-class service"
  • Generic superlatives or marketing clichés
  • The word "sustainability" — use "slow" / "local" / "ethical" instead
  • Mixed languages — copy is English only, even for Italian place names
04 · Reminders

Critical rules to respect in Canva

The few rules that are easy to break inside Canva if you're not paying attention.

Don't

  • Use pure white #FFFFFF — always use Warm White #FAFAF4
  • Put Stone Gold #C4920A as text on white — fails WCAG contrast
  • Use more than one Terracotta CTA element per layout
  • Use yellow or green tints as text colour — backgrounds only
  • Mix more than three fonts in a design (Young Serif + Lora + Fraunces is the maximum, and Fraunces is wordmark-only)

Do

  • Alternate section backgrounds (Ink → Parchment → Warm White → Sage…)
  • Pair every section title with a small uppercase eyebrow in Muted Ink
  • Keep Young Serif at one weight — vary size and tracking, not weight
  • Use italic Young Serif for pull quotes and H3 accents
  • Keep tracking tight on large serif titles (−0.02em at 40px+)
